ABOUT

Building better workflows for modern commerce.

Neo Party is building AI-powered tools that help online businesses transform product information into marketing campaigns, content, and repeatable creative workflows.

What we're building

Neo Party AI is an intelligent workspace where product context, strategy, content, and creative workflows work together. Instead of juggling disconnected tools for copywriting, campaign planning, and creative briefs, teams get one system that understands their products and generates structured, usable marketing outputs.

Why we're building it

Modern online businesses spend significant time repeatedly turning product information into descriptions, campaigns, social content, FAQs, and creative briefs. It's necessary work, but it's repetitive — and it pulls founders and small teams away from the work that actually grows the business. We believe AI should absorb that repetition, not add another tool to manage.

Our vision

"An intelligent workspace where product context, strategy, content, and creative workflows work together." We're early, and we're building in the open with the businesses who use the product every day.
